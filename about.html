About the Shore Conference

High school sports in Monmouth and Ocean counties.

The conference

The Shore Conference is the athletic conference for 48 high schools in Monmouth and Ocean counties, public and private.

Teams are organized into divisions by sport. Pick a team on a school’s page to see its division.

The conference runs its own tournaments. State tournaments are run by the NJSIAA.

History

  • 1936FoundedNine public high schools start the conference. Roy W. H. Maurer of Freehold High School is its first president.
  • 1940Twelve schoolsMembership is capped at twelve.
  • 1952A and B divisionsSchools are split into two divisions by size.
  • 1956Sixteen schoolsAsbury Park, Central Regional and Long Branch join.
  • Today48 schoolsPublic and private schools across both counties.
