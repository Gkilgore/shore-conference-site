About the schedules

The Shore Conference scheduling site brings school athletics calendars together.

School schedules

Each athletic director prepares and publishes their team schedules. Visit a school page for the latest published games, locations, and any listed bus times. Changes being prepared privately appear after the AD reviews and publishes them.

Conference calendar

A game appears on the conference calendar once both schools enter it and their game details agree. A game can appear on a school schedule first. If either school postpones or cancels a game, it stays on the conference calendar marked Postponed or Canceled.
