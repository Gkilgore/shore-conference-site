About the Shore Conference

The high school athletic conference for Monmouth and Ocean counties, New Jersey.

Member schools

The conference has 48 member high schools, public and private. Every one is in Monmouth County or Ocean County. See all 48 schools.

Schools are grouped into six divisions by location and size: A North, A Central, A South, B North, B Central and B South. Football uses its own divisions.

Teams play for division titles during the season, and the conference runs its own postseason tournaments. State tournaments are separate. They are run by the NJSIAA, the association that governs high school sports in New Jersey.

History

  • 1936Founded in FebruaryIt began as a league for the two counties’ Group Two public high schools, a state class based on enrollment. Nine of the eleven joined. Roy W. H. Maurer, athletic director at Freehold High School, was elected the first president. Teams competed in football, basketball, baseball and track.
  • 1940Capped at 12 schoolsThe conference turned down an application from Sayreville after limiting membership to twelve.
  • 1952A and B divisionsAs enrollments grew, the conference split into two divisions by school size, with the larger schools in Division A.
  • 1956Growth to 16 schoolsAsbury Park, Central Regional and Long Branch were admitted.
  • TodayPublic and privateFor decades the conference admitted only public schools. Its members now include private schools as well.

Source: History of the Shore Conference, on the conference’s athletic website.
