# Constitution content map

Reviewed November 16, 2026 for the Home and Information pages. The user-supplied primary source is the [constitution approved June 2, 2023](https://s3.amazonaws.com/shorec.sidearmsports.com/documents/2023/6/27/Shore_Conference_Constitution_Approved_June_2_2023.pdf). The conference later posted a [June 2024 revision](../assets/documents/SC_Constitution_2024_.pdf), saved in this repository. Its title page says “Revised June 2024.” No later approved constitution was located in the reviewed official records. Public governance wording therefore uses the 2024 revision and names its date. Public pages use the saved local copy.

The sourced timeline already covers 1950s divisions, the 1969 four-division alignment, 1960s discussion of girls' sports, and later non-public admissions. The June 2024 constitution describes present governance and is not used as evidence for rules in those earlier decades. The research dossier's citation tokens are not public citations; `data/history.js` links each published record to a named source.

The official site has a [February 2022 Rules and Regulations PDF](https://s3.amazonaws.com/shorec.sidearmsports.com/documents/2022/11/2/SC_Rules_and_Regulations_Feb_2022_Clean_.pdf). A current approved edition could not be verified. Do not label that 2022 file current or add it as a current-rules link until the conference confirms the governing edition and a saved copy is added through the document import process.
