Setup help

Pick your app. The games show up as their own calendar next to your personal one.

iPhone or iPad · Apple Calendar
  1. Choose Apple Calendar above and follow the prompts to subscribe.
  2. Give the calendar a name you will recognize, such as “Marlboro Girls Soccer.”
  3. If offered an account, choose iCloud to see it on your other Apple devices.
  4. Open Calendar and make sure your new calendar is checked in the Calendars list.

If the button doesn’t open Calendar, copy the link above. Then open Calendar → Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar and paste it.

Apple instructions, including older iPhones and iPads

Mac · Apple Calendar
  1. Choose Apple Calendar above. Or copy the link and open Calendar → File → New Calendar Subscription.
  2. Check or paste the link, then choose Subscribe.
  3. Name the calendar. Choose iCloud as its location if you want it on your other Apple devices.
  4. Choose an Auto-refresh interval and save.

Apple Calendar help for Mac

Android or iPhone · Google Calendar

Do this once on a computer. The Google Calendar phone app can’t add a calendar from a link.

  1. Copy the link above.
  2. Open Google Calendar on a computer with the Google account you use on your phone.
  3. Next to Other calendars, choose +, then From URL.
  4. Paste the link and choose Add calendar.
  5. Open Google Calendar on your phone using the same account. Open its menu and check the sports calendar so its games appear.

The Google Calendar button above starts this for you in a browser.

Google Calendar subscription help

Outlook
  1. Copy the link above.
  2. Open Outlook on the web with the account you use on your phone or computer, then open Calendar.
  3. Choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
  4. Paste the link, name the calendar and save.
  5. Make sure the sports calendar is selected in your calendar list. Check the same account in your Outlook app.

Microsoft subscription instructions

Another calendar app

Copy the link above. In your app, look for Subscribe, From URL or Add by URL, and paste it. An app that can only import files won’t get changes.

Games missing, duplicated or not updated?
  • Nothing appears: check that the calendar is turned on in your app and that you chose the right school, sport, team and level. Games appear once the school publishes them.
  • A change has not arrived: this site always has the latest schedule. Your app decides when to refresh, and some take a day or longer.
  • Games appear twice: you may have subscribed and also imported a file, or subscribed to both a school and one of its teams. Remove the extra one in your calendar app.
  • Want a different team: choose it above and subscribe. Remove the old calendar in your app.

You can’t edit subscribed games. To fix a game, contact the school’s athletic office.
