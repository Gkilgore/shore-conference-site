How to sync with your calendar

Choose your school and team above, then follow the steps for your app. Your sports schedule appears as a separate calendar alongside your personal events. You do not need to copy games into your personal calendar.

Choose a subscription to receive changes. Importing a downloaded .ics file gives you a one-time copy. After subscribing, new games and AD changes arrive when your app checks for updates.

iPhone or iPad · Apple Calendar
  1. Choose Apple Calendar above and follow the prompts to subscribe.
  2. Give the calendar a name you will recognize, such as “Marlboro Girls Soccer.”
  3. If offered an account, choose iCloud to see the subscription on your other Apple devices using the same account.
  4. Open Calendar and make sure your new calendar is checked in the Calendars list.

If the button does not open Calendar, copy the subscription URL above. On current iOS and iPadOS, open Calendar → Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar, paste the URL, then choose Find and finish adding it.

Apple instructions, including older iPhones and iPads

Mac · Apple Calendar
  1. Choose Apple Calendar above. Or copy the subscription URL and open Calendar → File → New Calendar Subscription.
  2. Check or paste the URL, then choose Subscribe.
  3. Name the calendar. Choose iCloud as its location if you want it on your other Apple devices.
  4. Choose an Auto-refresh interval and save.

Apple Calendar help for Mac

Android or iPhone · Google Calendar

Set this up once in Google Calendar on a computer web browser. Adding a calendar from a URL is not available inside the Google Calendar phone app.

  1. Copy the subscription URL above.
  2. Open Google Calendar on a computer with the Google account you use on your phone.
  3. Next to Other calendars, choose +, then From URL.
  4. Paste the subscription URL and choose Add calendar.
  5. Open Google Calendar on your phone using the same account. Open its menu and check the sports calendar so its games appear.

The Google Calendar button above offers a shortcut to start adding the selected calendar in your browser.

Google Calendar subscription help

Outlook
  1. Copy the subscription URL above.
  2. Open Outlook on the web with the account you use on your phone or computer, then open Calendar.
  3. Choose Add calendar, then Subscribe from web.
  4. Paste the URL, give the calendar a name, and save.
  5. Make sure the sports calendar is selected in your calendar list. Check the same account in your Outlook app.

Microsoft subscription instructions

Another calendar app

Copy the subscription URL above. In your app, look for Subscribe, From URL, or Add by URL and paste it. Your app must support calendar subscriptions to receive updates. If it only imports files, use a subscription-capable app such as Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook.

Games missing, duplicated or not updated?
  • Nothing appears: check that the sports calendar is enabled in your app and that you chose the right school, sport, team and level. An unpublished school cannot be subscribed to yet.
  • A change has not arrived: check the school calendar on this site for the latest saved schedule. Your app controls refresh timing; updates can take a day or longer in some apps.
  • Games appear twice: you may have both a subscription and an imported copy, or overlapping school and team subscriptions. Keep the subscription you want. Remove unwanted copies or subscriptions in your calendar app.
  • Want a different team: choose it above and add its subscription. Unsubscribe from the old calendar in your app if you no longer want it.

Subscribed games are read-only. Contact the school's athletic office to correct a game. Editing your own copy will not update the school's schedule.
