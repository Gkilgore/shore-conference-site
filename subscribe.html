How to add this calendar

iPhone, iPad or Mac: choose Apple Calendar, then confirm the subscription in your calendar app.

Google Calendar: use a computer web browser. Choose Google Calendar above, or open Settings → Add calendar → From URL and paste the link. The subscribed calendar then appears in the app on your phone.

Outlook: in Outlook on the web, open Calendar → Add calendar → Subscribe from web and paste the link.

Other apps: look for “Subscribe,” “From URL” or “Add by URL.” Paste the subscription URL above.
